# Code of Conduct

We are a small team and we want everyone to do their best work.

- Be respectful. Disagree about ideas, not about people.
- Give feedback that is specific and kind. Ask questions before assuming.
- Share credit and take responsibility. If you break something, say so and help fix it.
- Ask for help early. Nobody is expected to know everything.
- Keep competition with other teams friendly. No harassment, put-downs or poor sportsmanship.
- Keep content free of hate and of political positioning.

This project follows the code of conduct of the IBM Z Datathon 2026, organised by Shooting Stars Foundation. Concerns can be raised with any team member or with an SSF organiser.
